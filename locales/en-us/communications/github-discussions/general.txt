Say hello and tell us what you are building with Axa

Welcome! This is the place for conversation about Axa that does not fit anywhere else: introductions, use cases, opinions, and general chat.

A quick introduction from our side: Axa is an accessories library for Axum. We built it so that a service keeps its ordinary Axum `main.rs` and still gets the pieces every service needs, such as health checks, a safe middleware stack, problem-details errors, background jobs, and database crates for Postgres, MySQL, and SQLite.

## One question for you

**What are you building with Axa, or what brought you here?**

A sentence or two is plenty. For example: "An internal API on Postgres with sqlx, and I wanted readiness checks that work with Kubernetes."

Your answers matter more than usual here. Axa adds a capability once two services need it, so hearing what your service does helps decide what comes next.
