Show us what you built with Axa

This category is for sharing what you have made with Axa: services, integrations, demos, tutorials, and experiments. Small and unfinished work is welcome.

Every post here also helps the project. Axa adds a capability once two services need it, so seeing real services shows us what to build next.

## A template you can copy

```markdown
### What it is

One sentence.

### Why I made it

The problem or curiosity behind it, in two or three sentences.

### Show it

A screenshot, a short code sample, or a link to a demo or repository.

### How to try it

A few steps, or "it is private, but here is how it fits together".

### Which parts of Axa it uses

Such as the middleware stack, health checks, a database crate, or the job queue.

### One thing that was hard or surprising

### What I would like

Feedback, contributors, or other people's examples.
```

## How to take part

- Share your own project using the template above.
- Credit the work you built on.
- Reply to others with specific, kind feedback, such as what you would reuse or one question about how it works.

We would especially like to hear which parts of Axa you used, and which you left out or replaced.
