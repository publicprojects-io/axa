Welcome to Axa Discussions

Axa now has GitHub Discussions, a place to ask questions, suggest ideas, and share what you build with Axa.

[Axa](https://github.com/publicprojects-io/axa) is an accessories library for Axum websites, databases, microservices, and more. It is a library, not a framework: your `main.rs` stays ordinary Axum, and you call into Axa for a middleware stack, health checks, RFC 9457 error responses, configuration, background jobs, database crates, and graceful shutdown.

## What this means for you

- **Questions get answers in one place.** An answer that works can be marked as accepted, so the next person finds it quickly.
- **Ideas get discussed before code.** Talking through a proposal here is the first step of the [RFC process](https://github.com/publicprojects-io/axa/blob/main/docs/rfc/index.md) for major changes.
- **Real use cases shape the roadmap.** Axa adds a capability once two services need it, or once one service needs it to keep a security or regulatory control. Telling us what your service needs is how that happens.
